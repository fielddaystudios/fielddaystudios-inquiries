How it works

Simple process. Serious outcomes.

Every engagement starts with a conversation, not a proposal.

01

Reach out

Fill out the contact form or send me an email. Tell me what's going on — even a rough description is enough to start.

02

First conversation

We'll set up a 30-minute call. No pitch, no pressure — I want to understand your situation and see if I can genuinely help.

03

Scoped together

If we're a good fit, I'll put together a proposal. We refine it together until it reflects exactly what you need.

04

We get to work

Engagement kicks off with clear goals, defined milestones, and regular check-ins. No disappearing acts.
